SearchLauncher replaces browsing through your phone with fast, keyboard-centric search. Your home screen opens with a keyboard, and one bar finds everything: what is on your phone, and what is on the web.

Features:
* Search apps, app shortcuts, contacts, settings, snippets, and custom actions
* Open web results in the built-in browser, which blocks ads and trackers by default
* Swipe the search bar sideways to switch browser tabs, or up to see them all as live previews
* Reach your tabs straight from the home screen, without opening the browser first
* Save bookmarks under a title you choose, and find visited pages back from the search bar
* Use it as your default launcher or as a home screen widget
* Launch web searches through configurable shortcuts
* Keep frequently used apps close with favorites and usage-aware history
* Store and copy text snippets from the search UI
* Use calculator queries, phone numbers, emails, URLs, and smart actions
* Customize wallpapers, widgets, colors, and dark mode

Privacy notes:
* Web shortcuts and search suggestions are optional
* They may contact third-party search providers when enabled
* The browser's blocklist is downloaded from the StevenBlack hosts project
* Crash reporting is opt-in only
