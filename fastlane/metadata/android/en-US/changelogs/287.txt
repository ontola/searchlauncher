Search shortcuts now show the app they search in. Reddit, YouTube, Maps, Play Store and more appear with their real icon, and the key to type sits beside them: tap the letter and space to start searching inside that app. Type an app's name and its row offers the same key. Also recently: the calculator accepts ×, ÷, √, π and degrees, and every search shortcut scrolls sideways while you type, ordered by use or dragged into your own order.
