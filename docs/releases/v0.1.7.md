# SearchLauncher 0.1.7: recovers when the browser freezes

## What changed

- **The launcher no longer stays frozen.** The built-in browser (Gecko) runs in the same process as the launcher. When Gecko blocked the main thread, the home screen froze too and Home could not bring it back. A watchdog now notices when the main thread has been stuck inside the engine for 4 seconds, reports it to Sentry (only when crash reporting is on) and restarts the app. The next Home press opens the launcher again.
- **No crash loop on the same page.** After such a restart, or after a native crash or ANR while a page was on screen, that tab opens its address fresh once instead of restoring the exact saved state that may have caused the problem.

The F-Droid / legacy WebView build is unaffected.

Includes [PR #205](https://github.com/ontola/searchlauncher/pull/205).

## Updating

Same signing as 0.1.6. The GitHub `app-play.apk` uses the Google Play identity; the legacy `app-release.apk` keeps its separate signature and the WebView engine.

## Validation

The change passed CI (build and unit tests, including a new `HangVerdictTest`). The real freeze could not be reproduced, so the watchdog has not yet been seen to trigger on a device.
