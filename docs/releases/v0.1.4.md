# SearchLauncher 0.1.4: new contacts in search, Google sign-in in the browser

## What changed

- **New contacts are searchable right away.** Contacts added while the launcher was in the background could stay missing from search until a restart. The contacts observer now retries once permission is granted, returning to the launcher checks for changes, and an open search re-runs when contacts, calendar or downloads are re-indexed. Includes [PR #191](https://github.com/ontola/searchlauncher/pull/191).
- **Google sign-in stays in the browser.** Signing in to Drive in the built-in browser ended on "Could not open this page (Error 17)" because the redirect back to drive.google.com was handed to the Drive app. Page-initiated navigations now only leave for an app when they go to a different host than the one opened in the tab, so sign-in round trips finish in the browser while links to other sites still open their apps. Includes [PR #192](https://github.com/ontola/searchlauncher/pull/192).
- **Drive searches open in the browser.** The Drive app claims Drive search links but shows a Google error page for them, so the Drive shortcut now always searches in the browser.
- **Apps win ties with contacts.** A contact whose name matches as well as an app no longer outranks it, so searching "team" shows the Teams app first. A contact still wins when its name matches better. Includes [PR #194](https://github.com/ontola/searchlauncher/pull/194).

## Updating

The GitHub `app-play.apk` uses the same signing identity as Google Play. The legacy `app-release.apk` keeps its separate signature for existing GitHub/F-Droid installations.

## Validation

Unit tests cover the browser app-link rule, the Drive shortcut flag and the contact and app ranking ties. The contacts refresh fix has no automated test and was not checked on a device.
