# SearchLauncher v0.0.46

## Set an alarm from the search bar

Typing a clock time, such as `22:10`, `22.10`, `7:30pm`, `alarm 6.45` or `wekker 6.45 gym`, now shows **Set alarm for 22:10** at the top of the results. It opens your clock app's set-alarm screen with the time, and any label, already filled in. A clock time no longer offers to call or text it as a phone number; `call 22.10` still works when you mean it.

## Shorter onboarding

Onboarding no longer stops to ask whether your phone has separate notification and Quick Settings trays. It goes straight from the notifications swipe to the Quick Settings swipe on two-tray phones, or to the app drawer swipe on one-tray phones. The hint about opening Settings now shows the same settings icon as the search bar.

## Favorite apps from the app drawer

Long-pressing an app in the app drawer now offers **Add to Favorites** or **Remove from Favorites**, next to App Info and Uninstall.

## Validation

Formatting and store-copy checks, unit tests, and the release APK build ran in CI for each change. This release includes pull requests [#155](https://github.com/ontola/searchlauncher/pull/155) and [#156](https://github.com/ontola/searchlauncher/pull/156).

The GitHub APK updates the regular installation of SearchLauncher. The Play production release is subject to Google's review.
