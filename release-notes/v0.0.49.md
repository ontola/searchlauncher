# SearchLauncher v0.0.49

## Search inside the apps you know

Search shortcuts used to be coloured letters, which made it easy to miss that "r" searches Reddit. Now a shortcut whose app is installed shows that app's real icon: Reddit, YouTube, Google Maps, Play Store, Spotify, Google, Gemini, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Wikipedia, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and LinkedIn. The subtitle reads "Search inside Reddit".

Beside each shortcut sits the key that starts it: its coloured letter and a space key. Tap them to type the shortcut, or type the letter and a space yourself. When your query matched nothing, the keys carry it over, so tapping R turns "cheap flights" into a Reddit search for it.

## Discover it from the app

Type an app's name, such as "reddit", and the Reddit app row shows the same key. Tap the row to open Reddit, or tap the key to search inside it.

## Shortcut strip

In the sideways shortcut strip, each shortcut shows its app icon with the letter as a small badge in the corner. Shortcuts without an installed app, such as Add Calendar Item, keep their letter tile everywhere.

## Also recently

- The calculator accepts the home keyboard's math keys: × and ÷, square roots (√(4+5), 2√9), π, and degree marks such as 180°-45°.
- While you type, every search shortcut scrolls sideways, ordered by use. Drag them into your own order in Custom Shortcuts, or Reset Defaults to go back to sorting by use.
